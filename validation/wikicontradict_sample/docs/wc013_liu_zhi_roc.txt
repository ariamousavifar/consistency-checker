Liu Zhi (ROC)

Liu Zhi first fled to British Hong Kong, and later on made a living in Indonesia as a Chinese language teacher. In 1953, he was ordered to return to Kuomingtang-controlled Taiwan as a political adviser to Chiang Kai-shek. He was decorated with the Order of Blue Sky and White Sun. He died in Taiwan in 1972.
Liu Zhi died on the 15 January 1971 (aged 78) in Taichung, Taiwan
