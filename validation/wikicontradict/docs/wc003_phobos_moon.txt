Phobos (moon)

The model designed in 2015 supported the discovery that some of the grooves are younger than others, implying that the process that produces the grooves is ongoing.
In November 2018, following further computational probability analysis, astronomers concluded that the many grooves on Phobos were caused by boulders, ejected from the asteroid impact that created Stickney crater. These boulders rolled in a predictable pattern on the surface of the moon.
