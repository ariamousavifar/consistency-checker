Russian Constituent Assembly

During the election results for the Russian Constituent Assembly on the 12/25 November 1917 , the Socialist Revolutionaries (SRs) got 17,943,000 corresponding to 40.4%.
During the election results for the Russian Constituent Assembly on the 12/25 November 1917, while losing the urban vote, the Socialist-Revolutionary Party received around 57–58% (62% with their social democratic allies), having won the massive support of the rural peasantry who constituted 80% of the Russian population.
