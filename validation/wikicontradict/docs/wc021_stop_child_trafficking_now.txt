Stop Child Trafficking Now

The first walk of the Stop Child Trafficking Now organization took place in September 2011 in Augusta, Georgia, United States.
The Stop Child Trafficking Now organization organized annual walks to raise funds and awareness about the issue. In 2009, organizers claimed to have organized walks in 41 cities nationwide and hoped to raise over a million dollars. The group organized a protest at Phillips Square, Montreal, Quebec, Canada in September 2009.
