Antherina

Antherina is a monotypic moth genus in the family Saturniidae erected by William Elford Leach in 1815
The larvae of Antherina feed on oleander, privet, willows, beech, Liquidambar, Crataegus (hawthorns), grapevine, lilac, cherry, laurel, Forsythia, Rhus, Pistacia, apple, pear, plum and peach leaves, but foodplants differ from species to species.
