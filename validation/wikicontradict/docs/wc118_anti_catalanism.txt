Anti-Catalanism

Rafael Aracil, Joan Oliver and Antoni Segura considered that until 1951, the persecution of the Catalan language was "total"
With the defeat of Nazi Germany in 1945, the regime changed their image,[contradictory] which allowed the Orfeó Català to put on Catalan productions, and the publication of Catalan books, though only classic works; works aimed at young people were prohibited to limit the learning of the written language.
