Tyler Mane

In 2007, Tayler Mane played Michael Myers in Rob Zombie's remake of Halloween. After winning the role, he noted that he consecutively watched seven of the eight Halloween films (excluding the third because Michael Myers does not appear apart from in an ad for the first movie) to better understand his character. He is the tallest actor (6' 8") to portray the character
Tayler Mane height is 6 ft 9 in (2.06 m).
