Another Suitcase in Another Hall

Tim Rice and Andrew Lloyd Webber had already enlisted actress Julie Covington to sing the part of Eva in the Evita musical, hence they were on the lookout for other supporting vocal personnel. They found out about Barbara Dickson, who had recently starred in the Willy Russell musical John, Paul, George, Ringo ... and Bert, and had charted on the UK Singles Chart with her cover version of the David Whitfield and Frankie Laine song "Answer Me" (1976).
Barbara Dickson and her manager, Bernard Theobald, had a discussion with Tim Rice and Andrew Lloyd Webber about starring in the musical, but her voice was declared "too delicate" for singing the numbers on Evita. So they offered her one song which was not sung by Eva's character, and that was "Another Suitcase in Another Hall".
