6.100B (Introduction to Computational Thinking and Data Science) requires 6.100A.
6.1010 (Fundamentals of Programming) requires 6.100A and 6.100B.
6.1020 (Software Construction) requires 6.1010.

6.1060 (Software Performance Engineering) requires 6.1020, 6.1210, and 6.1910.
6.5060 (Algorithm Engineering) requires 6.1060 and 6.1220.

The prerequisite relation is transitive: if course A is a prerequisite for course B, and course B is a prerequisite for course C, then course A is a (transitive) prerequisite for course C.

No course is a prerequisite for itself (the prerequisite relation is irreflexive).
