Lake Maracaibo

Lake Maracaibo is deep in the south and shallow in the north. The northern half of Lake Maracaibo, which looks like a bottleneck, is 55 kilometers long. The southeastern edge of Lake Maracaibo basin with a flat bottom is steep and the northwestern edge is gentle. Lake Maracaibo is slightly salty due to the influence of tides, and the overall salinity of Lake Maracaibo is between 1.5 and 3.8%.
The Catatumbo River forms a bird-foot-shaped delta in the southwest of Lake Maracaibo basin, and the surface lake water in the delta has a salinity of only 0.13%. However, the intrusion of seawater from the mouth of Lake Maracaibo makes the salinity of the bottom lake water higher, reaching 0.2-0.3%.
