Nymphaea nouchali var. caerulea

The underwater rhizomes of nymphaea nouchali var. caerulea are edible. Like other species in the genus, nymphaea nouchali var. caerulea contains the psychoactive alkaloid aporphine (not to be confused with apomorphine, a metabolic product of aporphine).
Apomorphine is said to be main psychoactive compound present in nymphaea nouchali var. caerulea. Other compounds include nuciferine.
