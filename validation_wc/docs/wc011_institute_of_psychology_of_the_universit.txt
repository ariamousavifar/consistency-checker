Institute of Psychology of the University of Paris

In 1965, Paul Fraisse becomes the Institute of Psychology of the University of Paris director
1952 : Paul Fraisse replaces Henri Piéron with a director board of the Institute of Psychology of the University of Paris including Daniel Lagache (Sorbonne, lettres), Jean Delay (Faculté de médecine), and Pierre-Paul Grassé (Sorbonne, Sciences). 1960 : Daniel Lagache steps back from co-directorship. 1961 : Paul Fraisse becomes the only director of the Institute of Psychology of the University of Paris.
