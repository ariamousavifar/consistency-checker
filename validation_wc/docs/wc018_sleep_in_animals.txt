Sleep in animals

Sleep in fish is the subject of ongoing scientific research.Typically fish exhibit periods of inactivity but show no significant reactions to deprivation of this condition.
If sleep were not essential in animals, one would expect to find  - Animal species that do not sleep at all - Animals that do not need recovery sleep after staying awake longer than usual - Animals that suffer no serious consequences as a result of lack of sleep. Hence sleep is essential for all complex animals.
