Kodimunai

A significant number of the residents of Kodimunai do jobs related to fishing. These jobs includes deep sea fishing, shallow water fishing, fishing from the shore (known as karamadi in the local language), fishing with mechanized boats, exporting fish, etc.
Many of the residents of Kodimunai work in a number of other fields like IT, medicine, education, engineering, trading, cargo shipping, etc. However, there is no noticeable local industry except for fishing
