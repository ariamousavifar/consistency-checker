Tregaron Estate

Joseph E. Davies and his wife bought the Tregaron estate in 1940. After Davies's death in 1958, the Washington International School purchased 6 acres (24,000 m2) of the 20 acres (81,000 m2), and the Tregaron Limited Partnership, an Israeli corporation, purchased the remaining 14 acres (57,000 m2).
The Tregaron estate covers 20.5 acres (83,000 m2).
