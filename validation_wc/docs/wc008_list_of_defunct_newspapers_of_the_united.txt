List of defunct newspapers of the United States

This is a list of defunct newspapers of the United States. Only notable names among the thousands of such newspapers are listed, primarily major metropolitan dailies which published for ten years or more.
The Hoover Gazette was a weekly newspaper that served the city of Hoover, Alabama. The Hoover Gazette was owned by Eagle Publishing Company LLC. The Hoover Gazette was first published on June 5, 2006. The last edition of Hoover Gazette was on August 15, 2007. The newspaper Hoover Gazette was published each Wednesday.
