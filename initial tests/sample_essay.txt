The Examined Life
A short reflection by an anonymous author

All philosophers question their own beliefs. Everyone who questions
their own beliefs is a seeker of truth. Socrates was a philosopher,
and all philosophers are human. Every human is mortal.

It follows that Socrates was a seeker of truth. It also follows that
Socrates was mortal, like the rest of us.

And yet I maintain that Socrates is immortal. His ideas will outlive
every empire that tries to bury them.

Some seekers of truth are never satisfied with easy answers.
